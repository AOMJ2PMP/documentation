## Night routine

- **8:00 PM**
  Journaling or brain dump (write tomorrow's to-do list to clear your mind)
- **9:00 PM - 9:30 PM**
  **Wind-down** — shower or wash face, brush teeth, change. This sequence signals your brain that the day is over.
- **9:30 PM**
  POI on the desk, enjoy :)
- **10:15 PM**
  10 min gentle stretching
  **Kindle or podcast in bed** — sleep timer set to 30 min. Low light only.
- **~11:00 PM**
  **Fall asleep** — naturally, without forcing it.
